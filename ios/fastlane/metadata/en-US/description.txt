SmartSpace helps you reclaim storage on your iPhone by intelligently identifying and removing duplicate photos, large videos, unused contacts, old downloads, and more.

KEY FEATURES

Photos Cleanup
Scan your photo library for duplicates, blurry shots, and screenshots you no longer need. Review suggestions side-by-side and free up gigabytes of space with a single tap.

Videos Cleanup
Find large and duplicate video files eating up your storage. Preview each video before deciding to keep or remove it.

Contacts Cleanup
Detect duplicate, incomplete, or outdated contacts. Merge duplicates and remove contacts with missing information to keep your address book tidy.

Downloads Cleanup
Remove leftover downloads and cached files that are wasting valuable space on your device.

Notification Manager
Take control of app notifications. See which apps are the noisiest and silence the ones you do not need.

Cloud Archive
Back up important files to the cloud before deleting them locally. Retrieve archived items anytime you need them.

Secure Vault
Protect your private photos and videos with biometric authentication. Keep sensitive files hidden and secure.

Smart Storage Dashboard
Get a clear overview of what is using your storage. The home screen shows your storage breakdown and gives personalized cleanup recommendations.

WHY SMARTSPACE?

- Fast & Lightweight: Scans complete quickly without draining your battery.
- Privacy First: All scanning happens on your device. Your files are never uploaded without your permission.
- Easy to Use: Clean, modern interface designed for everyone.
- Detailed Previews: Always see what you are about to delete before confirming.
