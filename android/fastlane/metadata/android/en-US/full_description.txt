SmartSpace helps you reclaim storage on your Android device by intelligently identifying and removing duplicate photos, large videos, duplicate contacts, old APK files, and more.

KEY FEATURES

Photos Cleanup
Scan your photo library for duplicates, similar shots, and screenshots you no longer need. Review suggestions side-by-side and free up gigabytes of space with a single tap.

Videos Cleanup
Find large and duplicate video files eating up your storage. Preview each video before deciding to keep or remove it.

Contacts Cleanup
Detect duplicate or incomplete contacts. Merge duplicates and remove contacts with missing information to keep your address book tidy.

APK & Downloads Cleanup
Remove leftover APK installer files and old downloads that are wasting valuable space on your device.

Notification Manager
Take control of app notifications. See which apps send notifications and open each app's notification settings to silence the ones you do not need.

Secure Vault
Protect your private photos and videos with your fingerprint, face or a PIN. Keep sensitive files hidden and secure.

Smart Storage Dashboard
Get a clear overview of what is using your storage. The home screen shows your storage breakdown and gives personalized cleanup recommendations.

WHY SMARTSPACE?

- Privacy First: All scanning happens on your device. Your files are never uploaded without your permission.
- Easy to Use: Clean, modern interface designed for everyone.
- Detailed Previews: Always see what you are about to delete before confirming.
